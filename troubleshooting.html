Troubleshooting and FAQ#

Most problems come from three things: the Expansion ATM being off, a player on an older version than the server, or a market edit that needs a restart.

Problem Fix
F8 says "Not an admin" Add the Steam64 ID it shows to Admins in Settings.json and restart
Shops say the ATM is off, or rent never charges Turn on ATMSystemEnabled in Expansion's MarketSettings.json. Rent pauses while it's off; nothing is deleted
Online pay not arriving Check F8 → SETTINGS → ONLINE PAY is on (1) with an amount above 0
Parking zones not on the map Check ShowMapMarkers is 1 and that players have the same version as the server
No shop markers ShopMapMarkers must be 1, and Expansion's EnableServerMarkers on in its map settings
Restock doesn't show at the trader Close and reopen the trader menu. Stock is per zone, so every trader in that zone changes
Can't place the shop kit Use flat, open ground, inside your territory if Expansion limits building to territories
Shop container vanished F8 → SHOPS → VIEW → RESPAWN CONTAINER. Stock and funds are safe in the shop file
Market edits don't show Restart the server; Expansion reads market files only at start-up
Courier or zone PvP not working on a PvE server Load @GhostzSuite after PvE mods such as Ninjin's PvP/PvE Bundle
No message sound Tablet → HOME → SETTINGS → New message sound may be set to OFF
Server won't start after updating to 1.20 Remove @GhostzQoLPlus from -mod: it's built into Expansion Plus now
Workshop app says it's switched off Turn the module on in F8 → ALL TOOLS → QOL ADMIN CONTROL → DASHBOARD (General.json → Modules)
Gear Appraisal says it needs Expansion Market The Expansion Market must be loaded and its trader files readable
Loot scanner "recharging" Wait for the cooldown shown in Quick Status (default 10 minutes)
"No answer from the server" in a Workshop app Wait a moment and try again; check the server log for [GQP] lines
Stack sizes didn't change The stack patch must be packed, loaded last on server and clients, and the server restarted
Something else Check profiles/GhostzSuite/GhostzSuite.log, profiles/GhostzQoLPlus/Logs/ and the server script log, then post them on Discord

FAQ#

Can players fake money or items? No. Every price, payment, stock change and spawn is checked and done on the server.

What happens to shop stock on a restart? Nothing. Stock lives in the shop's file, not in the world.

Do I need to wipe to update? No. Settings and data carry over; new settings appear with their defaults. See Updating to 1.20.

Where do Workshop fees go? They're paid with Expansion Plus money (bank or cash, the player's choice) and logged in Transactions.

Do players need the update too? Yes. The tablet, map drawing and sounds run on each player's game, so players must have the same version as the server.
