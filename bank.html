Player features

Bank and online pay#

Tablet → Banking. The bank is the player's Expansion bank, the same money the ATMs use. No ATM is needed to use it from the tablet.

Accounts#

  • Bank limit: 500,000,000 for player and group accounts (setting BankMaxBalance). Expansion's own default is 100,000; Expansion Plus raises it at start-up and checks it every minute.
  • DEPOSIT CASH / WITHDRAW CASH anywhere.
  • Savings – a second account paying 10% every 24 hours, compounding. PUT IN SAVINGS / TAKE OUT.
  • Group account – one per Expansion group. OPEN GROUP ACCOUNT, then any member can PAY IN or TAKE OUT, and the group sees the last moves.
  • SEND MONEY – find any player, online or offline, and send them money from your bank.
  • TRANSACTIONS – every payment in and out with date, time and type.

Loans#

  • Lend – put money up as loan offers, held from your bank. Post as many as you like, ADD AMOUNT to one, or WITHDRAW it.
  • Borrow from players – every offer, cheapest first. Take one for 1, 2, 4 or 7 days and pay it back with 35% on top.
  • Borrow from the bank – up to 100,000, with 50% on top, for the same times.
  • PAY BACK NOW at any time. Otherwise, on the due date the money is taken from your bank, then your savings.
  • Can't pay? What you have goes to the lender, then a random vehicle from your garage is lost for good (a player lender gets it in their garage). With no vehicle in your garage, your whole inventory is taken (at your next login if you're offline).
  • You're warned an hour before a loan is due. One running loan per borrower by default.

Online pay#

Players are paid into their bank for time online.

Setting Default
Pay players for time online On
Pay per hour online 500
Pay out every 60 minutes (30 = half the hourly pay every 30 minutes)
Tell the player On

Each payment shows in the bank transactions as Online pay. Admins change these in F8 → SETTINGS → ONLINE PAY.

Note

If another mod on your server also pays for time online, turn one of them off so players don't get two messages.

For admins#

F8 → BANKING: every rate and limit, banking on or off, bank loans and group accounts on or off, a savings cap, totals, running loans (with CANCEL DEBT) and the bank log. Saved in profiles/GhostzSuite/Bank.json.
