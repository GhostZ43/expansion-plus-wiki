Trade

Player shops#

Trade → Shops / My Shop. Players run their own shops that other players buy from and sell to.

Opening a shop#

  1. Buy a Player Shop Kit (a small wooden crate) from a trader.
  2. Take it in your hands and use Place shop. A preview follows your cursor (within 12 m), sitting on the terrain, with an arrow out of its front (green = fine, red = not here).
  3. Turn it with the mouse wheel or Q / E, then left click to place it. Right click or Esc cancels and you keep the kit.
  4. It becomes a shipping container shop that's yours, with a marker on every player's Expansion map. Choose the marker's name and colour under MY SHOP → MAP MARKER.

Money: bank transfers only#

  • Customers pay from their bank, and sellers are paid into their bank. Cash is never used.
  • Everything customers pay goes straight into the owner's bank.
  • Every shop starts with 100,000 in funds for buying items from players. Starting funds can be spent but never withdrawn.
  • Owners move money with BANK → SHOP and SHOP → BANK.

Stock and listings#

  • Stocked items leave the map and are saved in the shop's file. A buyer gets a fresh item in their inventory (or at their feet).
  • Accepted items: Worn or better, empty, nothing attached, magazines unloaded, no spoiled food.
Field Meaning
Sell price What customers pay for one (0 = not for sale)
Buy price What the shop pays for one (0 = not buying)
Buy up to The shop stops buying at this much stock
  • STOCK / TAKE at the shop moves items between your inventory and the shop.
  • MAIL TO SHOP sends items from anywhere into your shop (not within 60 seconds of combat).
  • WHOLESALE buys stock from the Expansion market at 15% off (20% off for 10 or more).

Trading#

  • SHOPS lists every shop with what it sells and buys, its rating and location, with tabs by Expansion trader.
  • Open a shop from any side: stand by the container, look at it and press F.
  • Buying and selling only work at the shop (within 4 m). Anywhere else, the shop is view-only.
  • Rate a shop 1 to 5 after trading with it. TOP SHOPS ranks them.
  • PACK UP SHOP (empty and at the shop) removes it and gives the kit back.
