Reference

Settings reference#

Every setting is editable in game (F8 > SETTINGS, or GARAGE ADMIN > SETTINGS for the garage) and is saved to profiles/GhostzSuite/Settings.json or profiles/GhostzSuite/Garage.json. Switches: 1 = on, 0 = off.

Main settings (Settings.json)#

Player shops#

Setting Key Default Notes
Player shops ShopsEnabled 1 1 = on, 0 = off
Starting funds per shop ShopStartingFunds 100000 for buying from players - can never be withdrawn
Shops per player ShopsPerPlayer 1 how many shops one player may own
Items per shop ShopMaxListings 40 different items one shop can list
Most stock of one item ShopMaxStockPerItem 1000 per item, per shop
Shop markers on the map ShopMapMarkers 1 yellow marker with the shop name (1/0)
Shops in TOP SHOPS ShopTopCount 10 how many the ranking shows
Mail to shop ShopMailEnabled 1 owners send items from anywhere into their shop (1/0)
Wholesale WholesaleEnabled 1 owners buy stock from the Expansion market at a discount (1/0)
Wholesale discount (%) WholesaleSinglePercent 15 below the market price
Bulk discount (%) WholesaleBulkPercent 20 when buying the bulk amount or more
Bulk amount WholesaleBulkQty 10 how many of one item count as bulk
Mail fee per item ShopMailFee 0 taken from the bank, 0 = free
No mailing after combat (s) ShopMailCombatSeconds 60 after taking a hit or firing, 0 = no wait
