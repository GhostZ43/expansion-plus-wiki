Garage

Garage#

Garage → Garage (EP Garage Services), or press K. Store your vehicles safely, take them out where you need them, and buy, sell, repair and insure them.

Storing and taking out#

  • STORE VEHICLE stores a vehicle of yours within 12 m, with its parts, fluids, damage and cargo exactly as they were.
  • Park vehicle (garage) in the scroll menu parks the vehicle you're looking at, with the same rules.
  • TAKE OUT brings it back at a spot you aim at, 4 to 15 m away (boats need water and get double the range).
  • Vehicles come out with the fluid levels they were parked with, standing on top of slabs, ramps or floors (never under them). A vehicle that drops through the ground in its first 5 seconds is put back.
  • Combat lock: no parking within 30 seconds of a hit or shot, or with an enemy within 100 m.
  • Each player can own 10 vehicles by default, counting every vehicle they own anywhere.

Parking zones#

Admins can require parking and taking out to happen in parking zones or at your territory flag. Parking and dealer zones show on the Expansion map with the parking icon, the zone's name and a ring round the zone, but they don't clutter the map legend. You get a message when you enter or leave one.

Locks and ownership#

  • Lock vehicle / Unlock vehicle in the scroll menu (look at the vehicle, press F).
  • Auto lock after the owner has been more than 10 m away for 60 seconds.
  • A locked vehicle offers nobody Get in, and its doors and inventory can't be opened from outside. Admins can still get in.
  • A vehicle nobody owns becomes its first driver's when they sit in the driver's seat.
  • Works on cars, boats and the DayZ 1.30 motorbikes.

Admins can switch to Expansion car keys (VehicleLockSystem = keys) or turn locks off (off).

Sharing with your group#

Group sharing is on by default: anyone in the owner's Expansion group can take out, store and drive the owner's vehicles, including ones a teammate stored. Selling, giving and insuring stay with the owner. You can also share a vehicle with up to 5 other players. Admins can turn group sharing off (NoPartySharing) to go back to per-vehicle sharing.

Dealer and player market#

  • Dealer – buy and sell vehicles at Expansion market prices at any trader (50 m). Damage and missing parts come off the price.
  • Player market – list a stored vehicle for other players to buy (3 listings each by default).
  • Give a vehicle to another player.

Services#

Service What it does
Service Body repair, worn part repair, fluid top-up, ruined part replacement
Valet Frees a vehicle tied to a zone (10,000)
Bring vehicle Brings your vehicle to you (25 m radius, 60 s cooldown)
Repair callout Fully repairs a broken-down vehicle where it is (25,000, fuel not included)
Insurance 25% of the value; get the vehicle back if it's destroyed

Impound and towing#

Admins can turn on a parking meter (unused vehicles get impounded) and towing (vehicles left outside parking areas get towed after 120 minutes unused, with a fee and a warning). Impounded vehicles are released from the garage for their fee.

Taxi and dashboard#

  • TAXI takes you to a stop or a spot on the map: see Taxi.
  • Garage → Dashboard shows the vehicle you're in: see Vehicle Dashboard.
  • Cruise control and the boat radar are on Getting started.
