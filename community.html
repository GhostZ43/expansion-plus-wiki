Trade

Donations, players and server info#

Donations#

Trade → Donations. Near any trader (50 m), donate items you don't need, or take one free item every 5 minutes. You can't take back your own donations. A DONATIONS button also appears at the top of the trader screen.

Leaderboards and reviews#

  • Leaderboards rank every player by rating, kills, K/D, deaths, infected, animals, longest kill, time played or courier deliveries, with a search.
  • Reviews – give another player 1 to 5 stars and a comment, after 30 minutes played. One review per player; saving again replaces it.

Server info (F9)#

Press F9 (or Social → Server Info on the tablet):

  • Players online, server time and the next restart.
  • Welcome, rules, wipes, restarts and events.
  • Links (Discord, website, store and the Workshop page) that open in your browser. The same links are on the tablet's Links button.
  • Your stats and the top 5 players.

Admins edit all of it in F8 → INFO.

Notifications#

Social → Notifications turns pop-ups for messages, bank and bounties on or off, each with its own sound. Saved on the server for your account.
