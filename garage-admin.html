Admins

Garage admin#

F8 → GARAGE ADMIN (or Garage → ADMIN on the tablet).

Tab What admins do there
PLAYER GARAGES Open any player's garage: spawn a vehicle here, release, unstick, free service, give
IMPOUND Impounded vehicles and their fees; release
TOW LIST Vehicles due to be towed
ZONES Add parking, no-take-out and dealer zones where you stand; set type and radius
TAXI Stops (add, name, fare, MOVE HERE, teleport, remove), fares, ride-anywhere options, points of interest, NO-TAXI AREAS
CLAIMS Insurance claims, lost vehicle files
SETTINGS Every garage setting

Parking zone kinds#

Kind Effect
Parking (local) Park and take out here; with "take out where parked", vehicles come out only here
Parking (shared) Park here and take out at any parking area
No take-out Vehicles can't be taken out here
Dealer The vehicle dealer works here

Parking marker icon#

ZONES or SETTINGS → CHANGE ICON opens a scrolling list of every Expansion icon, shown in your marker colour, with a search. SELECT changes the parking marker on everyone's map at once. The marker colour is set with Marker red / green / blue in SETTINGS.

Parking and dealer zones are drawn on every player's Expansion map with the icon, the zone name and a ring. They never appear in the map's marker list. Turn on 3D markers to show them in the world too.
