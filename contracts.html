Jobs

Courier contracts#

Jobs → Contracts (EP Courier Co.). Carry a crate from a pickup to a drop-off for pay.

Doing a job#

  1. Pick a job from the job board. Pay is per km of the route (4,000 per km by default).
  2. TAKE JOB costs a 10% deposit, returned on delivery.
  3. The Courier Cargo Crate appears at the pickup. Carry it, or put it in a vehicle (not tents, barrels, clothes or the garage).
  4. Put it down within 30 m of the drop-off, look at it and use Deliver cargo (F).

The time limit is 4 minutes per km, plus 10 minutes.

Everyone sees the crates#

Every running crate shows on every player's map and in the world, with the pay and the courier's name. Everyone is told when a job starts, is delivered, stolen or failed.

Robbing and PvP#

  • Anyone can deliver someone else's crate and gets 70% of the pay; the courier loses the deposit.
  • Whoever carries a crate, rides in its vehicle or stands within 2 m of it can be hurt by other players anywhere, PvE and safe zones included.
  • Whoever hits such a player loses their own PvE protection for 120 seconds.
  • Members of the same Expansion group still can't hurt each other.

Note

Load @GhostzSuite after @Ninjins-PvP-PvE-Bundle and any other PvE mod, so courier PvP works.

Player jobs#

Post your own delivery: the pickup is where you stand, PICK ON MAP sets the drop-off, then set the pay and POST JOB. The pay is held from your bank and a crate appears for you to fill. CANCEL refunds you before anyone takes it.

Quests and missions#

Jobs → Contracts → QUESTS AND MISSIONS:

  • Every Expansion quest you can do, your running quests, their objectives and rewards.
  • Start and cancel quests from the tablet. Hand in finished quests too, if the admin allows it (off by default), with one button per reward choice.
  • Quest money rewards go straight into your bank.
  • Running Expansion missions (airdrops, AI missions) with difficulty, location, distance and time left.

Admins set these in F8 → SETTINGS → QUESTS AND MISSIONS.
