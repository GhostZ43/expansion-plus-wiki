Garage

Vehicle Dashboard#

Garage → Dashboard. Sit in a vehicle and open the dashboard to see:

  • Condition of the vehicle.
  • Fuel, oil, brake fluid and coolant levels as bars.
  • PARTS STATUS: every part slot, installed, MISSING or damaged.
  • OPEN VEHICLE CARGO opens the vehicle's cargo in the Inventory app.

Admins choose whether passengers can use it, and whether fluids and parts are shown (Vehicles.json).
