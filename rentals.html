Garage

Vehicle rental and tracker#

Vehicle rental#

Garage → Rentals (EP Rent-a-Ride).

  • Stand within 50 m of any trader or inside any Expansion trader zone, or rent straight into your garage and take it out later.
  • RENT is always clickable and tells you why if you can't rent where you are.
  • Admins choose which market vehicles can be rented, for how long and at what price.
  • Rentals come out full: fuel, oil, brake fluid, coolant and a charged battery, with every part, and a paired key if the server uses Expansion car keys.
  • You're warned 5 minutes before the time runs out. When it ends, the vehicle is removed, so don't leave loot in it.
  • Your rentals show on your map. One rental at a time by default.
  • Every rental is logged step by step in the server's script log.

Vehicle tracker#

  • Garage → Tracker. Sit in the driver's seat and press TRACK THIS VEHICLE.
  • Tracked vehicles appear on your Expansion map and follow the vehicle.
  • Each one can be a 2D marker (map only) or a 3D marker (map and in the world).
  • Tracking stops by itself when the vehicle is parked, towed, impounded or destroyed, and you're told why.
  • Up to 3 tracked vehicles by default. STOP removes one.
