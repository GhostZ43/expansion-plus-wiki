Finance

Survivor Stock Exchange#

Finance → Stock Exchange (or Banking → OPEN EXCHANGE) opens a trading terminal with 24 fictional companies, each with a logo, industry, risk rating, description and dividend.

How prices move#

  • Prices move every market hour (60 minutes by default), even while the server is empty, and catch up after a restart (up to a week).
  • Each step moves a share at most 4%, and a share stays between 0.25× and 2.5× its company's normal value.
  • Chernarus Business Wire: 2–3 big stories a week. Their effect spreads over hours and is never guaranteed, and rival companies feel it too.
  • Market-wide booms, recessions and crashes.

Investment account#

Pay in from your bank and take out to your bank. Orders cost a small fee (0.5% by default). Dividends are paid weekly. Limits by default: 100,000 per deposit and per order, 2,000,000 in one account.

Note

The server decides every price and trade. Orders are checked, limited and handled one at a time.

For admins#

F8 → STOCK EXCHANGE: every setting, publish a news story, trigger a crash or boom, update now, edit or delist companies. Settings are in profiles/GhostzSuite/Stocks/Settings.json (reference).
