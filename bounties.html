Jobs

Bounties#

Jobs → Bounties. Admins turn bounties on in F8 → SETTINGS → BOUNTIES; they're off by default.

  1. Post a bounty on any player: a reward from your bank (5,000 to 10,000,000), a reason and a time limit (1 to 48 hours).
  2. New bounties go to every player's inbox with a notification.
  3. A hunter takes it by paying a 10% deposit.
  4. Kill the target and the hunter is paid reward + deposit. Run out of time and the bounty fails.

On the map#

Who Marker
The wanted player Red skull, seen by everyone (3D for the hunter)
The hunter Green skull, seen by the target and the poster

Admins choose what shows: the red skull to everyone or only to the hunter and poster, the green skull, or neither. Markers move every 5 seconds. The hunter and target can fight each other even on PvE servers. Each bounty shows a picture of the target in their gear.

One-life hunters#

If the hunter dies, the target keeps the hunter's deposit and the bounty opens again for someone else. If time runs out, the poster gets their reward back.

Discord feed#

Admins can post new, taken, paid-out and ended bounties to a Discord channel by webhook (each on or off).
