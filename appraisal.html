Workshop

Gear Appraisal#

Workshop → Appraisal shows what your gear is worth at the server's Expansion traders:

  • INVENTORY VALUE: the total.
  • BY CATEGORY: subtotals.
  • ITEMS (HIGHEST VALUE FIRST): every item with its value, and how many items no trader buys.
  • RECALCULATE refreshes it.

Prices come from the Expansion market on the server. Admins choose whether attachments and items inside containers are counted (Valuation.json).
