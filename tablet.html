Getting started

The Ghostz Tab 10.1#

Press F7 to pick up the tablet. It's a physical device: an aluminium rim, black glass, Ghostz Tab 10.1 printed on the top bezel with the camera beside it, power and volume buttons on the top edge, and three keys on the bottom bezel.

Back, Home and Forward#

Key Does
◁ Back Goes to the previous screen. Inside an app, its own sub-panel closes first
○ Home Goes to the home screen
▷ Forward Goes to the screen you just came back from

The keys light up under the mouse. History covers the home screen, hubs, every app page (Bank, Garage...), the Workshop apps and admin pages. Esc also goes back inside an app.

Home screen#

  • Status bar: clock, your bank and cash, and whether rent and rentals are paid from BANK or CASH.
  • Header: your name and live water, temperature, health, food and stamina squares, refreshed every 2 seconds.
  • Left menu: Home, Settings, Help, About, Links, Logout.
  • Hubs: one big tile per hub. A hub with only one app opens that app straight away (for example the Loot Scanner).
  • Quick Status: a strip with anything worth acting on, such as new messages, crafted items ready to collect, or the loot scanner ready or recharging.
  • Stat bars along the bottom.
Hub Apps
Finance Banking, Stock Exchange
Garage Garage, Rentals, Tracker, Dashboard
Workshop Inventory, Crafting, Repair, Drying, Appraisal
Trade Shops, My Shop (shop buildings), Donations
Jobs Contracts, Bounties
Territory Territories, Zones
Intel Loot Scanner
Social Messages, Notifications, Leaderboards, Reviews, Server Info
Admin The admin panel (admins only)

Apps an admin has locked are hidden from players (admins see them marked (locked)). Hubs with no apps left are hidden.

Workshop apps inside the tablet#

The Workshop apps (and the Loot Scanner and Vehicle Dashboard) open inside the tablet's own screen: the header shows the app's name, messages appear in the status bar, and the bezel keys work as usual. F5 opens the tablet on the Workshop hub.

Settings#

Left menu → Settings holds each player's own choices:

  • Background – 12 pictures to choose from, including Misty Peaks and Dragon Dusk (new in 1.21).
  • Pay from bank or cash – SWITCH BANK / CASH sets where rent, rentals and Workshop fees are paid from first. Player shops always use bank transfer.
  • New message sound – OFF, the Expansion Plus chime (default) or one of 24 game sounds. PLAY previews a sound.

Tip

The background and message sound are saved in your own game, so they stay the same on every server running Expansion Plus.

Links#

Left menu → Links opens the Expansion Plus Workshop page and the server's own links (Donate, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Twitch, Kick and any others). Admins set them in F8 → INFO → TABLET LINKS BUTTON.

Fits every screen#

The tablet is designed at 1080p and scales to 1440p, 4K, ultrawide and smaller screens.

3D views#

  • Garage → vehicle → 3D VIEW shows the vehicle with its parts fitted, plus owner, condition, value, fluids, parts and cargo. Drag to turn, mouse wheel to zoom.
  • Shops and donations have a 3D button on each item with its weight, size, capacity and description.
