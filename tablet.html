Getting started

The tablet#

Press F7 to open the Expansion Plus tablet. Its HOME screen has a tile for every feature the server has turned on. Each app opens its own branded screen (EP Bank, EP Garage Services, EP Retail, EP Land Registry, EP Messenger and more), and HOME at the top of any app brings you back.

Apps#

App What it opens
Banking Bank, savings, group account, transfers, loans
Garage Your vehicles, the dealer, the player market, services
Shops / My Shop Browse player shops; run your own
Territories Rent for your territory
Messages Messenger, contacts and admin calls (unread count on the tile)
Contracts Courier job board
Bounties Post and hunt bounties (when admins turn it on)
Zones Territory control zones
Rentals Rent a vehicle near a trader
Donations The free item box near traders
Leaderboards / Reviews Player stats, rankings and reviews
Server Info The F9 info page
Backgrounds Pick the tablet's background picture
Admin The admin panel (admins only)

The home screen also shows your water, food, health, stamina and temperature, refreshed every 2 seconds.

Tablet settings#

HOME → SETTINGS holds each player's own choices:

  • Tablet background – CHANGE picks one of 10 pictures.
  • Pay from bank or cash – SWITCH BANK / CASH sets where rent and rentals are paid from. Player shops always use bank transfer.
  • New message sound – CHANGE opens a scrolling list (mouse wheel or UP / DOWN). Pick OFF, the Expansion Plus chime (the default) or one of 24 game sounds. PLAY previews a sound; clicking a row picks it. It's used for messages, admin answers and bounty alerts.

Tip

The background and message sound are saved on your own game, so they stay the same on every server running Expansion Plus.

3D views#

  • Garage → vehicle → 3D VIEW shows the vehicle in 3D with its parts fitted, plus its owner, condition, value, fluids, every part and its cargo. Drag to turn, mouse wheel to zoom.
  • Shops and donations have a 3D button on each item showing its weight, size, capacity and description.
