Territory

Territory control zones#

Territory → Zones lists every zone, its kind, income, holder and status. Admins set zones up in F8 → CONTROL ZONES.

Income#

Zones pay their holders every 24 hours, online or offline, straight into the bank:

Kind Income per 24 hours
Military base 25,000
City 20,000
Town 15,000
Village 10,000
Industrial 5,000

Every extra zone a group holds adds +10% to all its zone income. Income is split between group members by default.

Production: zones can also make items each payout into the holding group's production store. Admins choose the items and amounts per zone kind.

Taking a zone#

  • Stay inside for 15 minutes with nobody from another group inside.
  • The holders being inside, or another group walking in, stops the takeover.
  • The holders are warned when a takeover starts, stops and succeeds, and every capture is announced to the server.

PvP inside zones#

Inside a zone, every player can be hurt by other players, ignoring PvE and safe-zone protection. Players get "PVP ZONE – YOU ARE VULNERABLE" when they walk in. Group members still can't hurt each other.

Zones are drawn on every player's map: a pin and a ring in the zone's colour, with its name, type and holder. They don't appear in the map's marker list.
