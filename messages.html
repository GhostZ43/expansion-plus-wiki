Player features

Messages#

Tablet → Messages (EP Messenger). The tile shows how many messages are unread.

  • ALL PLAYERS – everyone the server has seen, online first, with a search. MESSAGE, ADD CONTACT, BLOCK or REPORT anyone.
  • CONTACTS – your contacts, online or offline.
  • INBOX – your conversations, newest first (up to 300 characters a message).
  • A new message pops up a notification and plays your chosen sound. Offline players are told how many they have when they join.
  • BLOCK – blocked players can't message you. Unblock them under BLOCKED.
  • REPORT – sends the admins your reason and your last messages with that player.
  • CALL AN ADMIN – a subject and a message. Online admins are told at once. The answer arrives in your INBOX under ADMIN TEAM.

Messages and calls are deleted after 72 hours.

Tip

Change the new-message sound, or turn it off, in Tablet → HOME → SETTINGS → New message sound.
