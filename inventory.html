Workshop

Inventory Manager#

Workshop → Inventory. Your gear, nearby storage and the cargo of the vehicle you're in, as icon grids.

Sources#

The SOURCES list holds everything you can reach:

  • You: what you wear and carry (worn items count only for your own inventory).
  • Nearby storage: tents, barrels, crates and other containers within 3 m, in line of sight.
  • Vehicle cargo: the vehicle you're sitting in (drivers only while it stands still).

Stashes, other players and locked storage (vanilla and Expansion locks) are never listed.

Moving items#

  • Click items to select several, pick a target in TRANSFER TO, then TRANSFER >.
  • STACK ALL merges partial stacks.
  • SPLIT splits a stack.
  • SORT: by name, quantity or condition. Search by name.
  • REFRESH reloads.

The server checks reach, line of sight, locks and room for every item, one at a time, the same way the game's own inventory does.

For admins#

Inventory.json: radius (1–10 m), line of sight, nearby containers, vehicle cargo, nearby unlocked vehicles, drivers only while stationary, most items listed, and containers that are never accessible. See the reference.
