Garage

Taxi#

Garage → TAXI. Ride to a taxi stop or, if the server allows it, to any spot on the map.

Taking a ride#

  1. Click a stop on the map or in the list, or click any spot and press SET DESTINATION HERE → CHOOSE THIS SPOT.
  2. The fare and distance are shown. Press TAKE THE TAXI.
  3. Your vehicle within reach is parked in the garage first (at the nearest parking zone), if the server has that on.
  4. You're set down on solid, dry ground at the stop, never inside an object or in water.

Fare = the stop's fare (or the base fare) plus the price per km. There's a wait between rides (120 seconds by default).

Rides anywhere#

  • On by default. Rides under 300 m are refused.
  • No map rides within 2 km of an Expansion trader or a point of interest from the map's own place list (military bases, airfields, prisons and similar). Towns and villages are always fine.
  • Admins can add NO-TAXI AREAS where map rides can't end.

Note

Every check happens again when you press TAKE THE TAXI, before any money moves. Nothing is charged if anything fails.

For admins#

GARAGE ADMIN → TAXI: add stops where you stand or by typed X Y Z, name them, give each its own fare, MOVE HERE, teleport to them or remove them. Set the fares, ride-anywhere options, points of interest and no-taxi areas. See the taxi settings.
