Getting started

Expansion Plus#

Expansion Plus is an all-in-one add-on for DayZ Expansion servers. Banking, a vehicle garage, player shops, territory rent, vehicle rentals, messaging, courier contracts, control zones, bounties and a full admin panel, all on one in-game tablet.

What's in it#

  • Banking – deposits, savings with interest, group accounts, transfers, loans, and pay for time online.
  • Garage – store and take out vehicles with their cargo, locks, a dealer, a player market, repairs, insurance and parking zones.
  • Player shops – players place their own shop and trade by bank transfer.
  • Territory rent – territories pay daily rent by base size.
  • Vehicle rental and tracker – rent vehicles at traders; track your own on the map.
  • Messages – a messenger with contacts, blocking, reports and admin calls.
  • Courier contracts – carry crates for pay, steal other couriers' crates.
  • Control zones – hold zones for daily income.
  • Bounties – put a price on another player's head.
  • Admin panel – market editor, trader restock, map markers, wipes, logs and more.

Everything is checked and done on the server: players can't fake money, prices or items.
