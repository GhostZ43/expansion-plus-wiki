Getting started

Expansion Plus#

Expansion Plus is the all-in-one add-on for DayZ Expansion servers. Banking, a stock exchange, a full vehicle garage, player shops, territory rent, courier jobs, bounties, conflict zones, messaging and the Workshop survival tools, all on one in-game device: the Ghostz Tab 10.1. Admins run everything from the same tablet.

The hubs#

The tablet's home screen groups every app into hubs:

Hub Apps
Finance Bank, Stock Exchange
Garage Garage, Taxi, Rentals & Tracker, Vehicle Dashboard
Workshop Inventory, Crafting, Repair, Drying, Appraisal
Trade Player shops, Donations
Jobs Courier contracts and quests, Bounties
Territory Territory rent, Conflict zones
Intel Loot Scanner
Social Messages, notifications, leaderboards, reviews, server info

Safe by design#

  • The server builds every screen and checks every click. Players can't fake money, prices or items.
  • Every file is saved with a verified backup. Damaged files are kept aside, never reset.
  • Every charge and refund lands in the player's Transactions.
